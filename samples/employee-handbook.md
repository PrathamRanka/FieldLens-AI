# Sample Employee Handbook

## Expense reimbursement
Employees may submit reasonable business expenses with itemised receipts within 30 days. Manager approval is required before reimbursement. Travel expenses above INR 15,000 require director approval. Personal purchases are not reimbursable.

## Incident escalation
For a severity-one service incident, notify the on-call engineer immediately, open an incident channel, assign an incident commander, and provide an update every 30 minutes until mitigation. Customer-impacting incidents require a written post-incident review within five business days.

## Remote work
Teams may work remotely with manager agreement. Customer data must only be accessed through approved company devices and authorised systems.
