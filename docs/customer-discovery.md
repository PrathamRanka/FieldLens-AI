# Customer discovery worksheet

Use this before tailoring FieldLens to a real customer. Do not collect secrets or production data in a portfolio demo.

1. Which workflow consumes the most analyst or support-team time?
2. Which source systems contain the authoritative data? Who owns each source?
3. What does a correct answer look like, and who approves it?
4. What data is restricted by role, geography, contract, or retention policy?
5. What is the current baseline for time-to-answer, escalation rate, and rework?
6. What latency and availability targets matter to users?
7. Which actions must remain approval-gated?
8. How will the customer evaluate accuracy, groundedness, safety, and adoption?
9. What is the smallest safe proof-of-concept that can be measured in two weeks?
10. Which client engineering team owns integration, monitoring, and long-term operations?

Capture assumptions, decision-makers, acceptance criteria, risks, and follow-up owners. Never claim customer discovery occurred unless you actually conducted it.
